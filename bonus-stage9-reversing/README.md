\# PULSE-09 Research Agent Analysis



\## Category



Reverse Engineering



\## Difficulty



Medium



\---



\## Scenario



During the Operation PulseLock investigation, security analysts recovered an internal utility from a contractor workstation associated with suspicious activity.



The utility appears to be a legitimate research support tool. However, investigators believe hidden functionality exists within the application.



Your objective is to analyze the executable, understand its behaviour, recover the required authorization key, and obtain the challenge flag.



\---



\## Challenge Files



Provided file:



```text

research\_agent.exe

```



\---



\## Objectives



1\. Analyze the executable.

2\. Determine how authorization is validated.

3\. Recover the correct authorization key.

4\. Unlock the hidden functionality.

5\. Obtain and submit the challenge flag.



\---



\## Rules



\- Do not brute force the application.

\- Focus on reverse engineering and analysis.

\- Static analysis, dynamic analysis, and debugging are allowed.



\---



\## Skills Tested



\- Program analysis

\- Reverse engineering fundamentals

\- Runtime logic investigation

\- String reconstruction

\- Binary inspection



\---



\## Expected Outcome



Successful analysis of the program will reveal the correct authorization method and allow recovery of the hidden flag.



Good luck, Analyst.
