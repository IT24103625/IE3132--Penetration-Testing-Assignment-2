# PL-06 Progressive Hints

## Hint 1 – 5% Penalty
Normalize all timestamps to UTC before comparing events.

## Hint 2 – 10% Penalty
Correlate authentication records with web access logs to identify the sequence of suspicious activity.

## Hint 3 – 15% Penalty
Check file creation, upload, and deletion events after the successful login to reconstruct the incident timeline.
