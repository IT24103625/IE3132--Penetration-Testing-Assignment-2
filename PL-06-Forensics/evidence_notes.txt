OPERATION PULSELOCK
PL-06 - FORENSIC TIMELINE RECONSTRUCTION

Scenario:
The MedCore General Hospital research server has experienced
suspicious activity. Investigators recovered authentication logs,
web access logs, and file activity records.

Your task:
1. Identify the first confirmed successful SSH login.
2. Identify the affected user account.
3. Identify the source IP address.
4. Identify when the research export file was created.
5. Reconstruct the sequence of suspicious events.
6. Support your findings with evidence from multiple log files.

Important:
All timestamps must be converted to UTC.
Do not modify the original evidence files.

The PL-06 flag is awarded after successful timeline reconstruction.
